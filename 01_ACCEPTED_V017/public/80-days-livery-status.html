Symbol meaning and historical limits

  • One colored Japanese victory flag records one credited Japanese aircraft destroyed.
  • One bomb silhouette records one completed bombing mission or sortie in this marking convention.
  • Counts must come from one selected photographic state.
  • Different dates cannot be combined into a maximum-count reconstruction.
  • Obscured symbols remain unresolved until a human reviewer approves the count sheet.
  • Generated color boards and AI reconstructions are excluded from the evidence chain.
