## Status meanings

- `research`: references are being gathered or reconciled.
- `spec-ready`: evidence matrix, map specification, UV policy and acceptance checks are present.
- `bake-blocked`: production maps cannot be approved until the authoritative model and selected historical mission state pass their gates.
- `review-ready`: baked maps and screenshots exist and are awaiting upstream review.
- `approved`: upstream historical and technical review has approved a specific version.

## Registration rule

Registration does not change the repository's active production aircraft. It creates a controlled, reviewable livery package that can be activated by a later task after source-model, mission-state and approval gates are satisfied.
